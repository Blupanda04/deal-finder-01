How every price was checked

Each deal on this page was found and checked on Friday 27 November 2026 by reading the price at the store or in a deals roundup that names the store. The check time and the source are on every card. A discount is only listed when the sale price and the usual price were both seen — the percentage is computed from those two prices, never copied from a banner.

What the verdict means

  • NEW LOW The lowest price we have seen for this product, below its usual sale range.
  • STRONG DEAL A large, genuine cut (40% or more) on a product that rarely goes this low.
  • FAIR DEAL A real saving, but the product has sold near this price before.
  • ROUTINE SALE A small cut on a product that is often discounted — fine, not a rush.

Prices move fast on Black Friday. The store page is the final word — if a price has changed since we checked, the store's price wins. Links go to the store; we take no cut from them.
